MINI-FIT JR. 
1.0 SCOPE 
This Product Specification covers performance requirements for the MINI-FIT JR.  4.20 mm (.165 
inch) centerline (pitch) printed circuit board (PCB) connector series with Tin or Gold plating, and  
The MINI-FIT JR. connector series terminated with 16 to 28 AWG wire using Crimp technology with 
Tin or Gold plating.
